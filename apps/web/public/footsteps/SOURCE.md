Footsteps from Kenney, Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0); original License.txt included.

The fifteen Ogg files are unmodified recordings (five variants each of concrete,
grass and wood). The runtime adjusts filtering, pitch and level for asphalt,
ceramic tile and dirt, and uses faster, stronger impacts when running.
